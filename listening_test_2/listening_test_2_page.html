Before you start

This test is part of a research project at IIT Roorkee on protecting voices from AI speaker recognition. You will hear short recordings of read English sentences and answer three kinds of questions:

  1. Type what you hear for 4 short sentences.
  2. Same person or not: 14 pairs of short clips.
  3. Rate the sound quality of 9 clips from 1 to 5.

Please use headphones or earphones in a quiet place. On an iPhone, turn off silent mode. A short tone check at the start tells whether headphones are on. There are no trick questions; your honest impression is the data. Your name and answers are used only for this project and are locked so that only the researcher can read them. No voice is recorded and you can stop at any time.
